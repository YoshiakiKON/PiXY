Submission of “PiXY: Pixel-to-Stage XY(Z) Coordinate Converter for Offline Targeting in Microanalysis” as an Original Software Publication


Dear Editor,


We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled “PiXY: Pixel-to-Stage XY(Z) Coordinate Converter for Offline Targeting in Microanalysis” for consideration as an Original Software Publication in SoftwareX.

PiXY is open-source software that links image-based target selection with physical positioning on analytical-instrument stages (e.g., LA‑ICP‑MS, SIMS, EPMA, SEM‑EDS). It provides an integrated, GUI-based workflow from particle recognition (centroid extraction) on pre-acquired microscope/SEM images, through fiducial-based least-squares affine transformation (2D→3D), to instrument-ready export of stage coordinates. By enabling offline targeting before instrument time, PiXY reduces manual relocation workload, shortens non-measurement stage operations, and improves instrument utilization.
